# Code of conduct

Thinking Raven projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep harassment, discrimination and personal attacks out of every space we run, including issues, pull requests, discussions and social channels.

Report unacceptable behaviour by direct message to [@thinkingravenai on X](https://x.com/thinkingravenai) or [Thinking Raven on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/thinking-raven-ai/). Reports are handled privately and promptly, and maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
